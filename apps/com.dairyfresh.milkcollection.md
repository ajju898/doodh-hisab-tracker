Doodh Setu – Milk Collection Application 🐄🥛

Doodh Setu is a smart and easy-to-use Milk Collection Management Application designed for dairy farmers, milk collection centers, and dairy societies. The app helps digitize the entire milk collection process, making dairy operations faster, transparent, and error-free.

With Doodh Setu, dairy owners can record milk collection data, manage farmer records, calculate fat and SNF, generate reports, and send instant updates to farmers directly on their mobile phones.

Key Features 🚀

✔ Milk Collection Entry – Record daily milk quantity, fat, and SNF easily.
✔ Farmer Management – Maintain farmer details and milk records in one place.
✔ Automatic Calculation – Instant calculation of milk price based on fat and rate.
✔ Real-Time Data Sync – All data securely stored on cloud server.
✔ Farmer Notifications – Farmers receive milk collection details and payment updates.
✔ Reports & Analytics – Daily, weekly, and monthly reports for better management.
✔ Payment Management – Easy tracking of farmer payments and balances.
✔ Mobile Friendly – Simple interface designed for rural dairy operations.
