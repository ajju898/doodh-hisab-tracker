FarmKeep is the only app you need to easily manage all your animals, breeding, production, finances, and more. Whether you are a backyard chicken enthusiast, homesteader, hobby farmer, or small to mid size business, FarmKeep supports your needs and grows with you. Our features and services include:

Up to date animal records
Record animal information such as birth date, sex, and weight, along with a dedicated photo gallery. Track your animals, health, vaccines, activity, breeding history, pedigree and more. No matter what animals you have, you can easily manage them all in one place.

Easy breeding management
Automatically calculate gestation periods and incubation times for over 1200 species and breeds, including custom ones. Get notifications about important milestones like exam dates, due dates, incubation lockdown, and hatch dates.

Optimize yield with production tracking
With FarmKeep’s simple record keeping experience, it’s easy to track products such as eggs, milk, honey, wool, and more on a daily, weekly, or monthly basis so you can see trends over time and improve productivity of your animals or crops.

Simplify financial record keeping
Record sales transactions, categorize expenses, and stay on top of your farm's financial health. Gain valuable insights and identify top-performing products or track costs associated with livestock, feed, equipment, and more.

Multi-member farm
Invite your family, friends, farm hands, customers or fans to join your farm with different levels of access so they can contribute or just keep up to date with all your farm activities.

Tasks and reminders
Set tasks and reminders for yourself or others on your farm to get ahead of all your farm chores. Keep everything organized all in one place so if you need an extra helping hand, it’s easy to share your routine with anyone.

Accurate land mapping
Map areas on your property where animals live and crops are planted. Plan your pasture rotation and more to help you better manage activities throughout the year.

Cross platform
Available on all devices and desktop web at farmkeep.com
